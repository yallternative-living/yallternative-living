Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handmade salves, soaks, shipping, and custom orders.

Where do you ship from?

Straight out of Landrum, SC. Most orders ship in 1–3 business days. Anything still waiting on a batch is marked Coming Soon on its product page, with the batch date right there.

What's your return policy?

Opened salves, scrubs, and soaks are final sale for hygiene reasons. Unworn apparel and sealed items can be exchanged. Anything arrive wrong or damaged? Email us and we'll make it right.

Do you have unscented or sensitive-skin options?

We do. The Y'allternative Miracle Balm is unscented and essential-oil-free, made for skin that side-eyes fragrance.

Can I get something custom or in bulk?

Yep. Email us or send an Instagram DM with what you're picturing (gifts, market swag, wedding favors, whatever) and we'll figure it out together.

Where can I meet you in person?

Farmers markets and Pride events around Upstate SC and beyond. Check out our events page or follow our socials so you know exactly where and when we'll be set up next.
