Shipping & Returns

How we get our goods to you, and what happens if things go wrong. Straightforward answers, no nonsense.

Shipping Policy

Processing Times: All of our products are handmade in small batches. Order processing times vary by product, so please check the specific product description for the most accurate timeline. We work as fast as we can, but quality takes time.

Lost or Stolen Packages: Once we hand your package over to the carrier (usually USPS or UPS), it is quite literally out of our hands. We are not responsible for packages that are lost in transit, delayed by the carrier, or stolen after delivery. If you have an issue with delivery, please contact the carrier directly with your tracking number.

Incorrect Addresses: It is your responsibility to ensure the shipping address you provide at checkout is correct. We are not liable for packages sent to old, incomplete, or misspelled addresses. If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address, you will be responsible for the cost of re-shipping it.

Return & Exchange Policy

Final Sale Items: For health and hygiene reasons, all opened or used salves, scrubs, balms, and soaks are final sale. We cannot accept returns on body care items once the seal is broken.

Eligible Exchanges: Unworn apparel and completely sealed, unopened products can be exchanged within 14 days of delivery. To start an exchange, email us at y.allternative.living@gmail.com with your order number.

Exchange Shipping Costs: Unless we made a mistake with your order (e.g., sent the wrong item), the customer is responsible for all shipping costs on exchanges.

Damages and Issues: Did your order arrive broken, damaged, or completely wrong? That's on us. Email us within 7 days of receiving the order with photos of the damaged item and packaging, and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

Refund Timeline: If a refund is issued (for damaged or incorrect orders), it will typically be processed within 5–10 business days after we confirm the issue. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Questions?

If you're not sure about a policy, just ask. Email us at y.allternative.living@gmail.com or DM us on Instagram, and we'll sort it out.
