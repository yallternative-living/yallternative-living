The Fine Print, Sweetened

Privacy Policy

We don't like small print any more than you do, so here's what actually happens with your information when you spend time on this site: no jargon, no funny business.

Heads up: this is a plain-language privacy policy written for a small, handmade-goods business, not a document drafted by a lawyer, and not legal advice. It's meant to tell you honestly what this site does and doesn't do with your information. For ironclad legal compliance certainty, have an actual attorney review it.

Just Browsing?

If you're only here to look around, here's the good news: this site doesn't require an account, and it doesn't send anything about you to a server just for visiting. Two small things get saved, and both stay entirely on your own device:

  • Your theme choice (dark or light mode) is saved in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers which one you picked next time.
  • Your "Saved For Later" wishlist (anything you heart on the shop page) is also saved only in your browser's localStorage.

Neither of those ever gets transmitted to us, to a database, or to anyone else. Clear your browser data (or switch devices or browsers) and both reset, because they were never anywhere but your own machine to begin with.

What We Collect Directly

Beyond the local browser storage described above, the only information this site itself directly collects is the email address you choose to give us if you sign up for our email updates (more on that below). There's no other form on this site that collects contact info, account details, or anything else about you.

When You Buy Something

Checkout on this site runs through Snipcart, a third-party cart and checkout provider. Snipcart in turn uses a payment processor (Stripe) to actually handle your payment. That means your name, shipping address, email, and payment details are collected and stored by Snipcart and Stripe, not by us directly. We see your order information so we can make and ship your goods, but the sensitive handling (card numbers, etc.) never touches our own servers because we don't run any.

For the full details on how Snipcart and its payment partners handle your data, read Snipcart's own privacy policy: they're the ones actually storing and processing that information, so they're the most accurate source on it.

Our Email Updates

If you sign up for our email updates, the email address you provide is stored by our email newsletter service and used only to send you the updates you asked for. We don't sell your email address or share it with anyone outside of running that newsletter. You can unsubscribe any time using the link included in any email we send.

Shopping On Etsy

Some of our goods are also listed on our Etsy shop, and this site links out there as another way to buy. Once you click through to Etsy, you're on Etsy's platform and subject to Etsy's own privacy policy, which is separate from this one and out of our hands.

Fonts

This site's typefaces are hosted on our own server, not Google Fonts or any other third party. Loading a page doesn't send a request (or your IP address) anywhere else just to render text.

Cookies & Analytics

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own. We do run Plausible, a privacy-first analytics tool, to see basic things like how many people visited and which pages are popular. Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect or store any personal data, and can't track you across other websites. It just counts anonymous, aggregate page views. There's no visitor-level profile of you anywhere in it.

That said, the third-party services embedded on this site (Snipcart, Stripe during checkout, and Etsy once you click through) may set their own cookies or use their own tracking as part of how they operate. Those are governed by those companies' own policies, linked above.

Children's Privacy

This site and everything we sell is intended for adults, not for children. It isn't directed at anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from kids.

Your Rights

We're a tiny handmade business, not a compliance department, so we won't claim any formal certification here. But if you're in the EU, the UK, California, or anywhere else with specific data rights you'd like to exercise (access, correction, deletion, whatever it may be), email us and we'll do our honest best to help.

Questions, Or Want Something Deleted?

There's no self-serve portal or automated system here. Just us. If you have questions about this policy, want to know what we have on file, or want your email address (or anything else we hold) deleted, email y.allternative.living@gmail.com and we'll take care of it by hand.

Changes To This Policy

If how this site handles data ever changes (a new tool, a new feature, actual analytics down the line), we'll update this page to keep it accurate rather than let it go stale.
