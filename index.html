Our Story

Made By Hand, Made For Y'all

Y'allternative Living started the way most good things do around here: with a queer Southern woman who was tired of choosing between "self-care" and "actually affordable," so she made the stuff herself. Every salve, soak and scrub is stirred, poured and labeled by hand in Landrum, SC.

This is handmade self-care for the black sheep and the bold hearts, the folks who never quite fit the brand voice at the big self-care companies. Southern-raised, alt-inspired, and unapologetically queer, we make the stuff we wish existed for us.
