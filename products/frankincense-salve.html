How to Apply

Warm a pea-sized amount between clean fingertips and gently massage into cuticles, rough heels, elbows, windburned cheeks, or irritated skin as needed.

Storage & Shelf Life

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Shelf life is 6–12 months.

Patch Test Guidelines

Contains sweet almond oil (tree nut). Apply a dime-sized amount to the inner forearm and wait 24 hours. Discontinue use if redness or irritation occurs.
