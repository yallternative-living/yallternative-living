Scent Profile & Notes

Intensity: Medium (3/5)

How to Apply

Massage a dime-sized amount into temples, back of neck, shoulders, or soles of feet 15–30 minutes before bedtime.

Storage & Shelf Life

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct heat. Shelf life is 6–12 months.

Patch Test Guidelines

Apply a small dab to inner forearm 24 hours before first use to ensure compatibility with essential oils.
