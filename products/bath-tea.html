Moonlit Meadow Bath Tea

$12.00 – $24.00
Poured in Landrum, SC · Small-Batch Promise

Dried lavender, chamomile, rose petals, and oat straw in a reusable muslin bag. Drop it in a warm bath, let it steep five minutes, soak until the day stops mattering. Gentler on your tub than loose herbs, and the bag's good for two or three rounds before you compost the whole thing.

Scent Profile & Notes

Intensity: Subtle (2/5)
Top Notes

Wild Meadow Flora, English Rose

Heart Notes

Sweet German Chamomile, Oat Straw

Base Notes

Botanical Lavender Bud

Ingredients

  • Dried Lavender Buds
  • Chamomile Flowers
  • Oat Straw
  • Rose Petals
  • Epsom Salt
  • Baking Soda

Note: Comes with a reusable organic cotton muslin bag. Each bag is good for 2–3 soaks. Compost when spent.

How to Apply

Place 1 filled organic muslin tea bag into hot running bathwater. Steep for 5 minutes, then squeeze bag gently while soaking for 20 minutes.

Storage & Shelf Life

Keep unused tea bags in a dry, airtight jar or pouch. Compost spent botanicals after 2–3 uses.

Patch Test Guidelines

Pure dried botanicals and mineral salts. Test steeped herbal water on wrist if prone to botanical pollen sensitivities.
