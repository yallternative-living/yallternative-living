How to Apply

Scoop a nickel-sized amount onto damp hands. Work thoroughly into palms, knuckles, and cuticles in circular motions, then rinse clean with warm water.

Storage & Shelf Life

Keep lid closed between uses to prevent excess water from entering the tin. Shelf life is 6–12 months.

Patch Test Guidelines

Exfoliating scrub with pumice and pink salt. Test a small amount on hands before first heavy use; avoid open cuts.
