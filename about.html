Part Goth, Part Grit, All Heart

This is the part where we're supposed to sound real polished and corporate. Instead, here's the truth: one queer Southern girl, a stove full of salves, and a whole lot of us who never fit neatly into one box. Come on in.

Meet Savanna

Hey Y'all, I'm Savanna

I'm the hands, the nose, and most days the mouth behind Y'allternative Living. Every salve, soak, and scrub gets mixed, poured, and labeled by hand right in Landrum, SC. No factories, no focus groups. Just me, my kitchen, and an unreasonable number of mason jars.

The name isn't just a cute mash-up. Growing up in these foothills, you get handed sweet tea in one hand and a church potluck plate in the other, and if you're anything like me, you spend years thinking you have to pick a side: country or alternative, Southern or strange. Turns out Upstate SC raised a whole quiet army of us: goth kids working the peach stand, emo kids who still say "y'all," queer folks who never left because this dirt is home too. Backroads, porch lights, and sweet tea still run through everything I make here, eyebrow-raising parts included. Y'allternative Living is for every one of us who got tired of choosing.

What started as small-batch soaks and salves a little over a year ago, mixed on my stove and poured on my kitchen table, has grown into a whole little apothecary for the black sheep and bold hearts. You'll usually find me in person too, tabling at farmers markets and Pride events across Upstate SC and beyond. Showing up face-to-face is part of the job, not an afterthought. I'm queer, I'm proud, and I built this so people like me could smell incredible without shrinking to do it. Taking up space and smelling amazing was always the whole mission statement.

Out And About

Find Us In The Wild

The internet's great, but this business was built on face-to-face, the kind where you can actually smell the shea butter before you buy it. When the calendar lines up, you'll find our folding table somewhere in Upstate SC or beyond: a farmers market, a festival, a Pride event with a little extra glitter on the display.

We pop up at farmers markets and Pride celebrations across Upstate SC and beyond, like Upstate Pride's Colors of Pride, because taking up space in person matters just as much as taking it up online.

Follow @yallternativeliving on Instagram and TikTok for the real-time pop-up schedule. That's where we post exactly where the table's landing next.
